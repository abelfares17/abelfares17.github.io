International Mobility

Planned gap year abroad to work internationally — either a long dev internship or a job to self-fund the experience. Target destinations: Canada or Australia.

Gap Year — Work Abroad (Canada / Australia)

The goal is to take a one-year break in my engineering curriculum to work abroad in a real professional environment. This project is designed to build financial autonomy, strengthen professional English, and gain maturity through responsibility and cultural immersion.

Self-funded experience Professional English Real-world constraints Career clarity

Why a gap year?

  • Finance: earn and manage a real budget abroad
  • English: daily professional communication
  • Adaptability: autonomy, logistics, responsibility
  • Career: confirm a dev path or broaden options

I will document the experience (projects, learnings, outcomes) and integrate it into this e-portfolio.

Target destinations

Two options with different strengths: a dev internship track (Canada) vs. work-and-immersion track (Australia).

Option A — Canada (long dev internship)

Canada is attractive for its multicultural environment and dynamic tech ecosystem. I aim to secure a long-term software development internship where I can apply skills in programming, systems and software architecture in English.

  • Professional experience aligned with software engineering
  • International teams and workplace communication
  • Strong leverage for future internships & applications

Option B — Australia (work & cultural immersion)

Australia is ideal for a work-and-immersion gap year. Through working-holiday style opportunities, I can take a job to fully self-fund the experience while building resilience, autonomy, and adaptability.

  • Self-funded year + real-life constraints management
  • Deep cultural immersion in an English-speaking country
  • Personal growth that transfers directly to engineering projects
